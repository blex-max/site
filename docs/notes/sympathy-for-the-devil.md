# Sympathy for the devil

I have over the past year attempted to use AI quite regularly for the
small bounded implementation tasks, and it has led me to develop
an unexpected new sympathy for management.

Management and software engineers have a complex relationship.
In my experience the best software engineers tend to work at least
in part for love of the craft. But code craftsmanship is rarely-to-never
important for the business, possibly outside of some safety-critical domains.
The concerns of management are usually development speed, and,
sometimes, that the product shipped is functional to some standard.

Often in the course of product development management will be
presented with the choice to allow their engineers to do something right
the first time, or instead mandate that it is done the fast way. That they then
choose to do something the fast way seems almost inevitable,
and is a particularly Sysphean frustration.
